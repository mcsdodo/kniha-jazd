  • The page loads the list with get_paperless_invoices (vehicle, year). It loads again when the vehicle or the year changes, and on "Obnoviť z Paperless". The button is disabled while the list loads.
  • The backend reads the Paperless documents with the tag fuel or car, in pages of 100. It keeps the documents of the selected year: the year of receipt_datetime, else the year of the Paperless "created" date.
  • The page does not sort the list. The backend sends no ordering to Paperless, so the order is the Paperless default.
  • The tag sets the type: fuel gives ⛽ (Fuel), car gives 📄 (Other). A document with both tags is Fuel.
  • The backend ignores the vehicle (single-vehicle scope). Each vehicle sees the same documents and the same "Priradený" state.
  • "Priradený" (green) means a link to a trip exists. "Nepriradený" (red badge, red left border) means no link. The card does not say which trip holds the link.
  • "Potvrdené napriek nezhode" shows on a linked card when the user picked "Priradiť a potvrdiť" (frame 4b). "Zrušiť potvrdenie" clears the flag with revert_paperless_override, with no confirm step.
  • "Dátum:" shows the date and time from the custom field receipt_datetime, or "?" when it is not set. "Cena:" is the total_amount field. "Litre:" shows only for a Fuel card with litres set, else "-".
  • "Otvoriť v Paperless" opens <Paperless URL>/documents/<id>/ in a new tab. The app never edits the document.
  • "Prideliť k jazde" shows only on an unlinked card and opens the trip picker (frame 3). A linked card must be unlinked first ("Zrušiť priradenie", frame 5) before it can go to another trip.
  • The red nav count counts only the unlinked Fuel documents (count_unlinked_paperless_fuel_invoices). Here it is 1, though two cards are "Nepriradený". An assign or an unassign refreshes the count.

  • a. "Načítavam..." is plain italic text. The page has no skeleton loader.

  • c. Each other error shows the raw error text in a yellow box. The text is English, from PaperlessError in paperless.rs, with the prefix "Error: ". Examples: "Tag 'fuel' not found in Paperless", "Custom field '...' not found in Paperless", "Paperless returned status 401", "Network error: ...".

Zrušiť
  • The list comes from get_trips_for_paperless_assignment (document id, vehicle, year). The backend fetches the document from Paperless again and does not trust data from the page.
  • The info bar shows the title, the liters, the price in "EUR" and the date. A missing value shows "??", so an Other document shows "?? L".
  • The list holds all trips of the vehicle and year. The page sorts them by the distance between the trip date and the document date. With no document date, the order stays as the backend sends it.
  • A blue border marks a trip within 3 days of the document date.
  • "✓ zodpovedá dokladu": the document time is inside the trip, and the trip liters and price (Fuel) or the other costs (Other) are equal. It also shows when the trip has no value to compare and the time is inside the trip.
  • "~ rovnaký dátum": the trip has no value to compare, and the document time is on the trip day but outside the trip time. Step 2 then shows a mismatch (frame 4b).
  • "⚠ <reason>": the data differs. The reason is one of "iný dátum", "čas mimo jazdy", "iné litre", "iná cena", the pairs of these, or "všetko sa líši". The hover tooltip shows the values, for example "Litre: doklad 35.44 L – jazda 30.00 L".
  • A trip with no marker has nothing to compare and the document has no date.
  • A grey trip ("⚠ Jazda už má doklad o tankovaní") cannot take a document: it already has a Fuel document, and this document has liters. A trip holds a maximum of one Fuel document and any number of Other documents.
  • For an Other document, a trip that already has an Other document skips the price check: the backend adds the new amount to the trip.
  • A click on an active trip opens step 2. "Zrušiť", Esc or a click outside the dialog closes it. Other texts: "Načítavam jazdy...", "Žiadne jazdy na pridelenie.", "Nepodarilo sa načítať jazdy".

  • The radio starts on the type of the document: "⛽ Tankovanie (PHM)" for Fuel, "📄 Iné náklady" for Other. The user can change it.
  • a. With no mismatch, "Priradiť" calls assign_paperless_invoice with the type and mismatchOverride = false.
  • b. With a mismatch, the yellow box "⚠ Údaje nesúhlasia" lists the values that differ: the time ("Čas dokladu ... – jazda ..."), the liters and the price.
  • b. "Priradiť s varovaním" links the document with mismatchOverride = false. The grid then keeps its warning.
  • b. "Priradiť a potvrdiť" links it with mismatchOverride = true. The card then shows "Potvrdené napriek nezhode".
  • Grid effect of the override: it hides only the ⚠ "dátum/čas dokladu mimo jazdy" on "Začiatok" / "Koniec" of that trip, for that type, and removes it from the legend count.
  • The override does not hide the ⚠ "suma iných nákladov nesedí so súčtom dokladov" on "Iné €" (the trip total differs from the sum of its Other documents). The grid has no warning for a Fuel liters or price difference.
  • Fuel assign: if the trip has no liters, the backend copies the liters and the price of the document to the trip and sets "Plná" (full tank). If the trip has liters, it only links.
  • Other assign: the backend adds the amount to "Iné €" and appends the document title to "Iné pozn.". If the trip has no Other document and "Iné €" equals the amount, it only links (no double count).
  • "Zrušiť" and Esc go back to step 1. A click outside the dialog closes it.
  • On success the dialog closes, the list reloads and the toast "Doklad bol pridelený k jazde" shows. On an error the dialog stays open and an error toast shows (frame 5).

  • "Zrušiť priradenie" on a card opens this confirm. The confirm button calls unassign_paperless_invoice. "Zrušiť", Esc or a click outside closes it.
  • An unassign of an Other document subtracts the stored amount from "Iné €" and removes the title from "Iné pozn.", if the note was not edited.
  • An unassign of a Fuel document only deletes the link. The liters, the price and "Plná" that the assign copied stay on the trip.
  • The toasts show top right, one under the other. A green toast closes after 4 s, a red one after 6 s. A click closes a toast. The frame shows all toasts at once to save space.
  • The assign and the clear-override errors add the raw backend text after the colon, with the prefix "Error: ". This text is English. The unassign error has no detail.
  • In read-only mode each write fails. The assign and the clear-override toasts then add "Aplikácia je v režime len na čítanie. ..." from the backend. The unassign toast shows only its own text.
  • "Najprv vyberte vozidlo" shows when "Prideliť k jazde" runs with no active vehicle.

Open points (from the code, not fixed)

  • The card type comes from the tag (src-tauri/core/src/commands_internal/paperless_cmd.rs:83), but the picker check decides Fuel by the litres field (src-tauri/core/src/invoice.rs:81). A fuel document with no liters is checked as Other, so the picker does not grey out a trip that already has a Fuel document.
  • The radio in step 2 does not change the check. The mismatch box and the grey trips use the type of the document, not the chosen type (src/lib/components/TripSelectorModal.svelte:292). If the user picks "Tankovanie (PHM)" for an Other document on a trip that already has a Fuel document, the assign fails with "Trip already has a fuel invoice" (src-tauri/core/src/commands_internal/invoices.rs:146).
  • The comment on FUEL_INVOICE_EXISTS_ERR says the frontend translates it (src-tauri/core/src/commands_internal/invoices.rs:19-21). It does not: the toast shows the English text (src/routes/doklady/+page.svelte:108).
  • The mismatch detail lines are hard-coded Slovak, not i18n keys (src/lib/components/TripSelectorModal.svelte:169, :174, :180, :182).
  • The info bar shows "?? L" for an Other document, which has no liters (src/lib/components/TripSelectorModal.svelte:211).
  • An unassign of a Fuel document keeps the liters, price and "Plná" that the assign copied to the trip (src-tauri/core/src/commands_internal/invoices.rs:321-330, copy at :150-155).
  • The nav count counts only unlinked Fuel documents (src-tauri/core/src/commands_internal/paperless_cmd.rs:102). An unlinked Other document is "Nepriradený" on the page but is not in the count.
  • The backend ignores the vehicle (src-tauri/core/src/commands_internal/paperless_cmd.rs:54). Each vehicle sees the same documents and the same "Priradený" state.
  • The page has no read-only handling. The buttons stay active and the writes fail with a toast (src/routes/doklady/+page.svelte:1-17, no app-mode import).
  • Docs out of date: docs/features/paperless-integration.md:13 says "skeleton loader", the code shows "Načítavam..." (src/routes/doklady/+page.svelte:162). paperless-integration.md:17 and unified-invoice-picker.md:12 say "Priradiť k jazde", the label is "Prideliť k jazde" (src/lib/i18n/sk/index.ts:1014).
  • Docs out of date: unified-invoice-picker.md:14 says same-day trips are highlighted. The code highlights trips within 3 days (src/lib/components/TripSelectorModal.svelte:81-85). Line 15 of the same doc says an Other document on a trip with an Other document always gets "✓"; the code checks the time (src-tauri/core/src/invoice.rs:131-133).
